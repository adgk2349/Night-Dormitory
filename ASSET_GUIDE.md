# Asset Guide

Free assets are fine for this project, but every downloaded asset needs a license trail.

## Acceptable Sources

- Unity Asset Store free assets (Standard Unity Asset Store EULA: Free commercial use allowed).
- CC0 1.0 Universal / Public Domain (Freesound CC0, Kenney, ambientCG, Poly Pizza).
- Free commercial-use textures, models, and sounds with clear license pages.

## Required Notes Template

For each external asset, record:
- Asset name
- Source URL
- Creator or publisher
- License type
- Download date
- Any attribution text required by the license

## Optimization Target

Prefer:
- Low-poly models
- Small texture sizes, usually 512px or 1024px
- Repeated modular props
- Short compressed audio clips (`.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`, or small `.wav`)
- Simple materials over complex shader effects

Avoid:
- Huge photogrammetry packs
- Unclear "free" assets without license text
- Heavy post-processing
- Large animation packs unrelated to the demo
